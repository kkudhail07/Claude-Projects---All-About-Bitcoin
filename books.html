The reading shelf

Books

Six titles that, together, explain both Bitcoin and the money it's responding to. Start anywhere — each stands on its own.

Broken Money

Lyn Alden

A sweeping history of money through the lens of technology — why the current system is failing, and what better money could look like.

Get the book →

The Bitcoin Standard

Saifedean Ammous

The book that introduced "sound money" to a wide audience: a history of money and the case for Bitcoin as a decentralised alternative to central banking.

Get the book →

The Bullish Case for Bitcoin

Vijay Boyapati

The legendary essay, expanded into a book: a clear, structured argument for how and why Bitcoin could grow into global money.

Get the book →

The Price of Tomorrow

Jeff Booth

Why technology is naturally deflationary, why fighting that with ever-more debt is a losing game, and what an abundant future could mean for money.

Get the book →

Layered Money

Nik Bhatia

A short, elegant history of money told in "layers" — from gold and dollars to Bitcoin and central bank digital currencies.

Get the book →

The Big Print

Lawrence Lepard

A direct, passionate account of what relentless money-printing has done to ordinary people — and how sound money could fix it.
